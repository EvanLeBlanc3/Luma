Update the app you already installed

  1. Unzip LeLuma-iPhone.zip. Open the LeLuma folder.
  2. Open your existing GitHub repository. Choose Add file → Upload files. Upload everything inside the LeLuma folder, including icons and thumbnails. Replace the existing files and commit. The index.html file must be directly at the repository root.
  3. Wait for GitHub Pages to finish deploying. Your existing repository name and published URL can stay the same.
  4. Save any photo still open in the old app. Open the published HTTPS URL in Safari while online, wait a few seconds, and reload. Open Help and confirm LeLuma 1.3.0.
  5. If an old version persists, use Help → Repair app / refresh installed version, then tap its refresh button (it may still show the old name). It refreshes app files while preserving your saved Photos and favorites.
  6. An existing Home Screen icon may retain its old name. For the new name, open Safari’s Share → Add to Home Screen and name it LeLuma. Enable Open as Web App if shown. Open the new icon online once and wait for Offline ready.

First installation with GitHub

  1. Create a repository at github.com/new. Public works with the usual free GitHub Pages setup. You can call it leluma.
  2. Upload all contents of the LeLuma folder to the repository root, preserving the icons and thumbnails subfolders. Commit the upload.
  3. Open Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main → / (root) → Save.
  4. Wait for deployment, then open the exact published HTTPS link GitHub provides in iPhone Safari.
  5. Use Share → Add to Home Screen. Launch LeLuma online once, wait for Offline ready, then tap Open camera and allow access.

Camera preview recovery

This update separates video playback from filter rendering. A video pause no longer shuts down the stream, and a pending playback promise no longer locks the controls.

  • Resume video: starts playback directly from your tap when Safari pauses it.
  • Show camera only: shows the direct camera with your crop and selfie mirroring. Your selected filter is applied after capture. Use Show live filters to return.
  • Restart camera: releases and reopens the camera if no new frames arrive.
  • If it still fails, open Help → Camera details → Copy details and send that text with your published GitHub Pages link. It contains playback status, not photos.

The app has passed simulated media-state checks and actual pixel/export checks. Its camera behavior still needs verification on your iPhone.

Try the filters

Choose a category, then tap a filter. Each selection starts at 100% intensity. Adjust the slider afterward. Try Film → Noir for an obvious black-and-white check. The name above the image shows the selected look, and Look applied confirms its pixels have updated.

The Original toolbar button toggles before/after; choosing any filter exits that comparison. On the welcome screen, tapping a filter opens the sample scene so you can see its effect before opening your camera. The direct camera appears first, labeled Camera only · preparing look. The filtered canvas appears after a current frame finishes rendering. If filtering fails, the direct camera remains available with an explicit camera-only label and Retry preview.

Choose your frame

  • Full frame: preserve the whole image and all its dimensions.
  • Portrait 3:4: use a tall center crop.
  • Landscape 4:3: use a wide center crop.

The preview and saved photo use the same framing. You can change it after taking a photo. Framing keeps the pixels inside the crop without stretching or upscaling; the unfiltered original remains available in full. Controls stay upright, so you can make a landscape image while holding the phone upright. For a wider native landscape view, use Full photo or import a landscape original.

Take and save a photo

  1. Live shot captures the moment shown in LeLuma. Its source resolution is the video feed’s actual size. A 3- or 10-second timer is available.
  2. Full photo, the default, opens the iPhone camera for a higher-quality source. Take and accept the picture, then fine-tune it in LeLuma. LeLuma’s filters appear after returning from the native camera sheet.
  3. Tap Save photo, then Share / Save Image → Save Image to put it in Photos. If that option is unavailable, choose Open photo to save manually and touch and hold the image.
  4. Choose Keep an unfiltered original too to save a separate uncropped original.
Quality: Full frame preserves source dimensions; portrait and landscape deliberately crop edges. PNG avoids additional lossy encoding. Filters alter pixels, and filtered copies do not retain the native Camera app’s RAW, HDR, depth, Live Photo, metadata, or full color-processing pipeline. Keep your original. Very large photos can exceed your iPhone’s memory; the app reports an export failure rather than silently shrinking the picture.

The 25 new looks

Skin: Silk, Satin, Radiance, Velvet, Warm Balance, Soft Bronze, Fresh Light, Calm, Dewdrop, Natural Skin.

Definition: Sculpt, Athlete, Chisel, Form, Power, Bronze Edge, Feature, Precision, Contour, Steel.

Editorial: Cover, Runway, Spotlight, Espresso, Afterglow.

Skin looks soften skin-like colors; Definition looks use local contrast and sharpening to emphasize existing features, texture, and muscle contours. They do not identify faces, reshape anatomy, or add muscles. The original 50 looks remain available.

Offline and orientation

Open the app online once and wait for Offline ready. Its camera, filters and export work locally while its cached files remain on the phone. iOS may clear website data, requiring another online launch. Download iCloud-only photos before importing them offline. Save your photo before closing LeLuma; unfinished edits are held in memory.

The interface is designed to remain upright. Turn on Portrait Orientation Lock in iPhone Control Center for a firm screen lock. The Portrait/Landscape photo buttons choose image framing independently from screen rotation.

If something needs a nudge

  • Confirm Help says LeLuma 1.3.0 after replacing all repository files.
  • Use your published HTTPS GitHub Pages address in Safari. An HTML file in Files or a ZIP preview is not a camera installation.
  • If camera access was denied, set Camera to Allow or Ask in Safari’s settings for that website, then tap Open camera again.
  • If camera startup hangs, tap Cancel camera opening, then retry or use Full photo.
  • For an obvious preview check, use Film → Noir at 100% intensity, then tap Original to compare.
