A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Your pocket studio.

Choose your capture

Full photo opens the iPhone camera, then applies your look to the returned photo. This is the default for better source quality. Live filters are visible in LeLuma, not inside the iPhone camera sheet.

Live shot captures exactly when you tap. On iPhone, its resolution is usually limited to the live video feed; the actual pixel size appears above the preview.

Portrait or landscape

Choose Portrait 3:4 or Landscape 4:3 to frame your photo. The preview and saved picture use the same center crop. You can change framing after capture. Full frame keeps the whole image. The app’s controls stay upright; landscape framing does not require rotating your phone.

Every pixel, thoughtfully kept

Full frame exports retain the input dimensions. Portrait and Landscape crop the edges and keep the remaining source pixels without upscaling. PNG avoids extra lossy compression; JPEG at maximum quality makes smaller files. Filtered copies are standard color images, not RAW, HDR, Live Photos, or the original metadata. Keep your original for archival quality.

Looks that actually preview

Tap a look to apply it at 100% intensity, then adjust the slider. The Original toolbar button toggles before/after. A look’s name appears over the image; Look applied means its pixels have finished updating. Skin looks soften skin-like colors. Definition looks use local contrast and sharpening for features and muscle contours; they don’t reshape bodies or identify faces.

Keep your favorites close

Select a look and tap ★ Favorite. Open the always-visible ★ Favorites category to see your collection. Tap Favorited again to remove a look. Favorites and the open Favorites category are saved on this device between visits.

Smoother live filters

Live filters use hardware acceleration where available and follow incoming camera frames, targeting up to 30 frames per second. Preview detail adjusts automatically under load; your captured photo keeps its source dimensions. A compatibility preview is available if graphics acceleration fails, but may update more slowly. You can also use Show camera only and apply the chosen look after capture.

If the camera preview stops

Tap Resume video if playback is paused. Show camera only displays the direct camera feed; the selected filter is still applied after capture. Tap Show live filters to return to the filtered preview. If neither view moves, try Restart camera. Open your published HTTPS address directly in Safari and allow camera access.

Camera details

If you need help, copy these details along with your published app link. They contain playback status, not photos.

Save to your camera roll

Tap Save photo → Share / Save Image, then choose Save Image in the iPhone share sheet. If it is unavailable, open the photo and touch and hold it to save. Photos are not saved automatically.

Keep it upright

Enable Portrait Orientation Lock in iPhone Control Center. Safari cannot reliably lock the phone for us, so LeLuma pauses its controls when the phone is sideways.

Offline, and all yours

Open the app online once and wait for Offline ready. In Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen (enable Open as Web App if shown). Open it from that icon once online. Camera, filters, and exports then work without internet while the app remains cached. iOS can clear website data; open online again if needed.

No accounts, uploads, analytics, or external fonts. Pictures stay on your device. The current photo is held in memory: save it before closing or starting a new photo. Smoothing uses skin-like colors and can also soften similarly colored objects.
